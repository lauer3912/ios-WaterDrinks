## Description

Stay hydrated, stay healthy -- the complete water tracking solution.

WaterDrinks helps you build healthy hydration habits with smart reminders, beautiful progress tracking, and achievements that keep you motivated.

Core Features

- Daily Water Tracking -- Log your water intake with a single tap. Quick add buttons for 100-500ml.
- Smart Reminders -- Customize reminder intervals and active hours. Never forget to drink water.
- Visual Progress -- Beautiful circular progress indicator shows your daily goal completion.
- Calendar History -- Track your hydration journey with monthly calendar views.
- Statistics & Insights -- Weekly charts, average intake, and streak tracking.
- Achievement System -- 12 badges to earn, from "First Step" to "Hydration Hero."
- Dark Mode -- Fully supports dark and light themes.

Why WaterDrinks?

- Beautifully Designed -- Not another boring tracker.
- Offline First -- All features work without internet.
- Ad-Free -- No annoying ads.

Free download -- start your hydration journey today.
