Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026
Version: 1.0

WaterDrinks is designed to help you track your daily water intake and build healthy hydration habits. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

WaterDrinks stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • Water Intake Data: Your daily water intake records are stored locally using UserDefaults.
  • App Preferences: Settings like daily goal, reminder intervals, and theme preferences are stored on your device.
  • Achievement Progress: Your achievements and streaks are stored locally.

Data Usage

All data collected is used solely for the purpose of providing the WaterDrinks app functionality:

  • Tracking your daily water intake
  • Providing hydration reminders
  • Calculating statistics and achievements
  • Syncing data with the app widget (stored in shared App Groups container)

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. All your hydration data remains on your device.

Notifications

WaterDrinks sends local notifications to remind you to drink water. These notifications are generated locally and do not transmit any personal data.

App Groups

WaterDrinks uses App Groups to share data with the widget extension. This data stays within your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy

WaterDrinks does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
